title: "Small websites, kept well"
description: "A short checklist for keeping a site fast and readable long after launch day."
date: 2026-10-22
---

Launch day is the easiest day in a website's life. Everything after that —
the third year of plugin updates, the forgotten image folder, the banner
nobody remembers adding — is where sites go to get slow.

Our maintenance list fits on an index card: prerender what you can, measure
every quarter, delete more than you add. A site you can hold in your head
is a site you'll actually keep.

Posts here are sorted newest first. Settle in, and if something helps your
own project, pass it on — that's what the journal is for.
