---
title: "Designing at a walking pace"
description: "Why the studio ships weekly, keeps meetings short, and lets ideas cool before building them."
date: 2026-10-20
---

Most of our best decisions arrive on foot. A weekly build goes out every
Friday; by Monday morning we know what survived contact with real readers.
The rule is simple: nothing ships the same day it's dreamed up.

That pace looks slow from the outside. In practice it skips the two most
expensive mistakes — building the wrong thing beautifully, and rebuilding
the right thing twice. A notebook, a walk, and a week of distance do more
for a design than another round of same-day feedback.

- Weekly builds you can click, from the first Friday.
- One-page plans with a fixed price, no scope theatre.
- Meetings under half an hour, notes shared the same day.
